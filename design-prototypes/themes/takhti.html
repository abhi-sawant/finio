20 · Takhti

The writing board — the classroom blackboard and the shop’s price slate. Money worked out by hand, in chalk, where you can see the working; for people who want their sums human and informal.

ShapeNo cards. Rough outlines only where something is boxed (alert, keys, fields, “Near limit”): 1.6px strokes wobbled by an SVG turbulence filter, with chalk grain in dark and a clean marker line in light. Text is never filtered.
TypeKalam 400/700 for titles, labels, names and the big sums (68–96px answer, keypad); Nunito Sans 600/700 with tabular numerals for every list and table figure.
DensityMedium-airy. Sections sit open on the board, separated by single hand-ruled lines; rows are 54–64px and everything stays on a straight left edge.
MotionOn load the double underline under ₹1,562 scribbles itself, then the ring around today’s tally stroke is drawn — two stroke-dashoffset draws, about 0.9s in all. Nothing else moves.
VoiceA teacher’s working, only the real arithmetic: “₹40,000 budget − ₹15,000 spent = ₹25,000 left”, “₹25,000 ÷ 16 days =”. Product labels stay verbatim.
SignatureThe working shown: the sum written out to its double-underlined answer, beside a 31-stroke gate-of-five tally of the month with day 15 ringed; budgets and goals as diagonal-hatched bars with the percentage written at the end.
