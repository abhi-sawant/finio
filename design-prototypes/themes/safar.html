12 · Safar

The railway station. A departures board over the concourse at night, the yellow platform name board by day — for people who like their money as a timetable: what is due, when, and from which account.

ShapeBoards with 4px radii and hairline frames, flap tiles and keys at 3px, corner rivets on the hero board. Nothing glossy, no soft cards; rows are separated by thin rules.
TypeOverpass 800–900 for names and titles, Overpass Mono with tabular figures for every amount, date and board row. Caps only for column heads and status words.
DensityMedium-dense, strict columns: every list is a board with column heads (date, name, account, amount, tag) and 38–56px rows.
MotionThe hero figure flips in once: each tile drops its top flap, staggered left to right over about 1.2s, then rests. The keypad amount uses the same tiles; the caret blinks.
VoiceBoard wording in the gaps only, and only what the data says: “Day 15 of 31”, “Due in 3 days”, “On track”. The platform column shows the real account name.
SignatureSplit-flap figures with a hinge hairline, the amber “Due in 3 days” status row, 20-segment progress strips with a day tick, and a lit yellow tile for the active tab.
