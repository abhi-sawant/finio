11 · Tareekh

The wall calendar by the fridge. Big date numerals, Sundays in red, a note in every box — for people who think about money by the date: what came in on the 2nd, what is due on the 18th, what is left for each day ahead.

ShapeRuled rectangular modules packed edge to edge on shared 1px rules, each opened by a small filled header tab. 0px radii, no shadows, no gaps between modules.
TypeAnek Latin only, worked through its width axis: 75–80% condensed for labels and tabs (11–12px), 110–120% wide at weight 800 for date numerals and the ₹1,562 figure. Tabular numerals throughout.
DensityThe densest theme: hero, month grid, alert, accounts and latest all on one phone screen; budgets as four-cell ruled strips; desktop tiles seven modules with no dead gutter.
MotionDays 1–14 tick in left to right at 40ms a cell, then the red ring is drawn once around the 15th (stroke-dashoffset, 0.6s ease-out). Nothing else moves.
VoiceDates do the talking, and only true ones: “Day 15 of 31”, “Due in 3 days”, “₹1,000 left with 16 days to go”. Product labels stay verbatim.
SignatureThe month grid: October 2026 as a real Sunday-first calendar — 2 Oct carries the salary and the rent, today is ringed, and each of the 16 days ahead shows its ₹1,562.
