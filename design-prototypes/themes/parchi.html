6 · Parchi

The thermal receipt. Finio as the slip that curls out of a kirana-store billing printer — for people who trust a figure most when it is printed, itemised and totalled.

ShapeSquare everything, zero radius. Money sits on torn receipt strips with sawtooth top and bottom edges, laid on a darker counter. The tab bar is a perforated paper strip; the FAB is a square red print key docked into it.
TypeMartian Mono throughout, riding its width axis: 75% condensed for line items, 112.5% wide and bold for totals, which print double-height (scaleY 1.65) like a thermal head.
DensityDense like a bill — numbered line items, dotted leaders, dashed rules between sections and a double rule under every total. Figures stay 14px+ so they scan.
MotionThe hero slip feeds out of a printer slot once, line by line (steps() timing). Supporting: the add key and CTA give a short paper-jog on press; the caret is a blinking print block.
VoiceReceipt idioms in the gaps: line numbers, SUBTOTAL, BAL C/F, END OF SLIP, “THANK YOU · KEPT ON THIS DEVICE”. Product labels print as-is in capitals.
SignatureThe torn slip with its barcode and printed time stamp (15 OCT 2026 · 09:41, day 15 of 31) — and Budgets as an itemised bill whose NEAR LIMIT prints as an inverted red block.
