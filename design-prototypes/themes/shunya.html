4 · Shunya

Zero. Everything not carrying a figure is removed — no cards, no shadows, no fills. Just numerals, hairlines and a lot of air, for people who find other finance apps noisy.

ShapeNo containers at all. Structure is whitespace and 1px hairlines; the only filled shapes are the add button and the key moment’s dot.
TypeGeist at weight 200 for the figure (88px) and 300 for keypad digits; 400 for rows. Hierarchy is size and weight, never colour.
DensityAiry. Roughly half the rows per screen of Khata — long vertical rhythm, one figure per glance.
MotionQuiet: the month line draws in once, figures fade up 6px. Everything else is instant.
VoiceNeutral and brief. Labels are the product’s own words; nothing is celebrated or scolded.
SignatureThe month line: the days of the month as a hairline with today marked, and spend drawn on the same axis — you see pace, not just percentage.
