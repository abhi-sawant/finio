3 · Gulal

Holi-powder pop. Loud, sticker-bright and a little cheeky — for people who’d rather money felt like a game they’re winning than homework they’re behind on.

ShapeChunky 22px radii, 2.5px ink outlines and hard offset shadows. Things look cut from sticker paper.
TypeBricolage Grotesque at 800 for every figure and title — wide, friendly, a touch wonky. Figures run to 76px.
DensityRoomy and tappable. Big targets, one idea per block; built for a thumb, in a hurry, in a good mood.
MotionPhysical: keys press down into their own shadow, the burst badge wobbles, the hero sticker tilts in.
VoiceWarm and encouraging in the gaps (“you’ve got this”). Labels and figures stay exactly as in the product.
SignatureThe burst badge — budget used, stamped on the hero like a price sticker — and a colour per category.
