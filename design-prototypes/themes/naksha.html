13 · Naksha

The drawing sheet. Your month drafted to scale on a blueprint — every amount with an extent gets a dimension line — for people who want their money measured, not estimated.

ShapeNothing is filled. Panels are 1px drawn rectangles with 5px crop marks at the corners, 0 radius; section headers are ruled title-block strips; selection is a 45° hatch, never a solid.
TypeAzeret Mono 500–700 for every figure and annotation label (13–72px, tabular); Sofia Sans Semi Condensed for names and notes. Labels are lettered in caps, row names stay normal case.
DensityDrawing-sheet tight: 8px drafting grid, 1px rules between rows, no padding spent on cards. Desktop is one framed sheet with a legend column and a corner title block.
MotionDimension lines draw in once, left to right (0.55s ease-out), then their arrowheads appear. Nothing else moves; keys and buttons hatch on press.
VoiceDrafting notes in the gaps, all true to the data: “÷ 16 days”, “Day 15 of 31 · 48%”, “Scale 0 to ₹40,000”. Product labels are untouched.
SignatureDimension lines. The budget is a bar drawn to scale with arrowed spans for spent and left, a dash-dot datum at today, and the ₹1,562 callout on a leader. Housing at 94% gets a red-pencil revision cloud.
