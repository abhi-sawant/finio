2 · Khata

The accountant’s ledger. Columnar green paper, blue-black ink, a red margin rule — figures are set the way a bookkeeper writes them, rupees in one column and paise in the next.

ShapeNear-square. 2px corners, 1px ink rules, double rules under headings. Nothing floats, nothing is frosted.
TypeSource Serif 4 for every name and figure, Source Sans 3 for labels. Tabular, right-aligned columns.
DensityCompact — 40px rows, ruled like a page. The most information per screen of the five themes.
MotionPen-like and brief: tally strokes draw in left to right, the stamp lands with a 3° tilt. Nothing bounces.
VoicePlain bookkeeping. Totals are underlined once and double-underlined at the foot, as on paper.
SignatureThe ₹ | paise split column behind a red double margin rule, and a five-bar tally gate for every budget.
