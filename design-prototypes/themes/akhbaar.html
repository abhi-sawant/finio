9 · Akhbaar

The morning broadsheet. Your money set like the front page you read with your chai — a headline, a deck, a few tight columns — for people who like their numbers reported, not decorated.

ShapeNo cards and no radii. Structure is the newspaper grid: column rules, gutters, a heavy-and-thin double rule under the masthead and 2px section rules.
TypeUnifrakturMaguntia for the “Finio” flag only; Oswald 600 for headlines and the figure (88–100px); Newsreader with tabular numerals for every row, table and amount.
DensityPacked like page one: dateline, lead story, breaking strip, markets table and briefs on one phone screen; three ruled columns on desktop.
MotionThe banner headline is inked once — a left-to-right wipe that resolves from a slight bleed to sharp. Halftone bars then fill dot by dot.
VoiceHeadline voice in the gaps only, and only what the data says: “Due in 3 days”, “Spending runs behind the calendar”. Labels stay the product’s own.
SignatureThe masthead and dateline, the halftone pace chart (budget used against month gone), and accounts as a markets table with drawn triangles.
