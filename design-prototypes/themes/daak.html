18 · Daak

The post. Your money kept like correspondence on a pale-blue inland letter — every account a postage stamp, every day postmarked — for people who like things stamped, dated and filed.

ShapePaper rectangles with 0–2px corners. Panels carry the inland letter’s printed double border rule; sections are separated by dashed fold lines; fields are a label above a ruled line. Stamps and the tab bar have a real perforated edge (CSS mask).
TypeYoung Serif for titles, stamp values and the hero figure (56px phone, 84px desktop; the ₹ falls back to Public Sans because Young Serif has no rupee glyph). Public Sans with tabular numerals for rows and labels. Courier Prime only for postmark and dateline stamps.
DensityMedium and orderly, like a filled-in form: Home fits the hero, one notice, a three-stamp strip, the total and the latest entry; budgets are three ruled fields and a quartered bar each.
MotionOne moment: the postmark is struck on load — scale 1.25→1, rotate −14°→−9°, opacity 0→.85 with an ink-bleed blur resolving, 350ms ease-out. Keys and buttons only press down 1px.
VoicePlain. The only in-world words are true ones: the postmark reads “15 OCT 2026”, “THU” and “DAY 15 OF 31”; the notice stamp reads “DUE IN 3 DAYS”. No pin codes, no addresses.
SignatureThe postmark and the stamps: an authored double-ring date postmark with wavy cancellation lines in the hero corner, accounts as a strip of perforated definitive stamps, and a tilted double-bordered “NEAR LIMIT” rubber stamp.
