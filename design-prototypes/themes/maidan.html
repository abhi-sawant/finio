16 · Maidan

The cricket ground. A month’s budget read as a run chase — so much in hand, so many days left, and a required rate — for people who already read a scorecard without thinking.

ShapePainted tin. Figures sit on black number plates (2px radius, a hook notch and a top hairline) grouped in a white-framed board; lists are ruled scorecards with a 2px rule over the total row; buttons are squared at 4px.
TypeBig Shoulders Display 800/900 for the hero digits (56–84px), every plate and title; Barlow for rows, Barlow Semi Condensed for table figures and painted caps labels. Tabular throughout.
DensityScorecard-tight: 32px account rows, four stat plates across, hero board plus worm chart in the top half of the phone; desktop runs the board and a large worm across the top, three scorecards below.
MotionThe worm draws once left to right in 1.2s, then the ball lands on day 15 with one small bounce. Plates never animate; keypad plates depress 2px.
VoiceCricket idiom only where it is literally true: “₹4,355 under par at day 15”, “16 days to go”. No overs, wickets, teams or scores. Product labels stay verbatim.
SignatureThe chase: Safe to spend today as the required rate on digit plates, with Spent / Budget / Days / Left plates, and a worm chart of cumulative spend against a dashed par line to ₹40,000.
