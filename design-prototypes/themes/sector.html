17 · Sector

Chandigarh modernism. Raw concrete, a strict planning grid and enamel panels in pure primaries — for people who want their money big, blunt and gridded, with Swiss-poster clarity and tropical colour.

ShapeZero radius, zero shadow, no outlines. Everything is a flush rectangular block on a concrete ground, separated by fixed 6px joints; 8px margin to the screen edge.
TypeDarker Grotesque 800–900 at poster scale with leading under 0.8 (hero figure 128px on phone, 200px on desktop); Hanken Grotesk with tabular numerals for every row, label and amount.
DensityBig and blunt up top — the yellow hero owns a third of the phone — then tight 2px-jointed lists below. Desktop is an asymmetric 620 / 378 poster grid.
MotionLouvres open once on load: each bay’s enamel fill scales in from its left edge, staggered 60ms, ease-out. Keys and blocks flip to cobalt on press.
VoicePlain and brief. The gaps only say what the data says: “Day 15 of 31”, “owed”, “Solid is spent, open is left”.
SignatureThe façade: accounts as bays whose width is their balance, the card’s debt a true-scale red sliver; the month as 31 fins with today standing tall; budgets as bays split into spent and open.
