7 · Mahal

Bombay Art Deco after dark — Marine Drive facades, gilt sunbursts and terrazzo floors. For people who like their money presented with a little ceremony, and every figure still crisp.

ShapeStepped-notch corners on every panel with a gold outline and an inset keyline; the hero is an arched proscenium on ziggurat shoulders. Chips are portholes; buttons are elongated with chamfered ends.
TypeLimelight — a high-contrast deco display — for titles and the hero figure (64px, numerals stay open). Jost with tabular numerals for every row, label and keypad digit; inline labels in letter-spaced caps.
DensityCeremonial and symmetric: centred hero, centred date rules, generous rhythm. Fewer things per screen, each given a frame.
MotionThe sunburst fans open ray by ray on load (ease-out, staggered 22ms) and the pace needle swings into place after it; everything else stays still.
VoicePolished, a touch grand, like a cinema marquee — only in gaps: “Now showing · October 2026” lettered around the arch. Labels stay the product’s own.
SignatureThe sunburst gauge: a half-fan of gilt rays inside the proscenium, rays lit = share of budget spent, an ivory needle at today’s pace. Budgets get a small fan each; Housing burns vermilion.
