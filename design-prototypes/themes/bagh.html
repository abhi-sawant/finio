5 · Bagh

A kitchen garden. Soft, warm and a little hand-made — money you tend rather than police. Goals grow from seed to sapling, and the sun in your hero tells you how far through the month you are.

ShapeLeaf corners — three big radii and one tight one, flipped from card to card. Pebble buttons, floating tab bar, nothing sharp.
TypeGabarito (rounded geometric) for figures and titles, Figtree for reading. Warm, never technical.
DensityGenerous but not sparse: a comfortable, tactile middle. Rows breathe inside soft sheets.
MotionSlow and organic: the hero sun eases to its place, the budget fill grows with a leaf at its tip, keys sink like pebbles.
VoiceCalm and nurturing. Meaning is carried by growth stages and the weather, not by alarms.
SignatureThe month as a sun-arc in the hero, goals drawn as plants (seed → sprout → sapling), and budgets that grow from moss toward marigold.
