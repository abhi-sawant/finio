14 · Ajrakh

The hand block-printed cloth of Kutch and Sindh. For people who want their money app to feel made by hand — rich and warm, with pattern doing the decorating so the numbers can stay plain.

ShapeCloth rectangles with 2px corners. Every panel is a printed double line (1px outline, a second line 3px inside) with a 1px off-register colour beside it and an 8px lozenge border band on its top edge.
TypeRozha One for page titles, section heads, keypad digits and the 60–84px hero figure; Hind 400–700 with tabular numerals for every row, label and amount.
DensityMedium. Panels sit 8px apart on the patterned ground; rows are 40–54px. Pattern fills the gutters and any spare cloth, never a text area.
MotionOne moment: the hero’s stamped blocks press in one by one (scale 1.15 to 1 with opacity, 40ms apart, ease-out). Keys and buttons dye solid when pressed.
VoiceThe product’s own labels. In the gaps only what is true: “Day 15 of 31”, “38% used”, “Each block is 5% of the limit”.
SignatureThe stamped row: 20 eight-point blocks, each 5%. Inked blocks are spent, faint resist outlines are left, a turmeric marker is today. Housing at 94% stamps its last blocks in vermilion.
