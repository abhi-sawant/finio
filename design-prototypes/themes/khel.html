10 · Khel

The 8-bit handheld. Money as a small pixel adventure — budgets are HP bars, goals are quests, the add button is a spinning coin — for people who stay on top of things when it feels like play.

ShapeNo radii anywhere. Panels are RPG text boxes: a stepped outer pixel border with notched corners and a second inner line. Keys and buttons sit on a 3px base and drop 2px when pressed.
TypePixelify Sans for titles, labels, tabs and row text; VT323 for every figure (hero 76px, keypad 32px, row amounts 22px) because its 3, 5 and 8 never collide. Silkscreen only for tiny uppercase HUD tags (≥11px). Font smoothing is off on figures and titles only.
DensityChunky and HUD-like: big targets, boxed groups, accounts as three inventory slots so Home fits in one screen without scrolling.
MotionSignature: the hero score ticks up to ₹1,562 once on load (digit strips stepped, no easing) and the coin FAB idles with a 4-frame spin. HP bars fill one segment at a time; the selected category’s cursor arrow blinks.
VoiceGame idioms in the gaps, always true to the data: “Day 15/31”, “38% used”, “! 3 days”, “Quest: Emergency Fund 20%”. Product labels stay as they are.
SignatureThe budget as a 20-segment HP bar with a pixel heart — it turns red and the heart blinks near the limit — and the Add screen’s chunky pixel keypad with an “A” action button.
