15 · Kolam

The rice-flour drawing made fresh on the threshold each morning: a grid of dots and one line looped around them. For people who want calm, order and the small daily satisfaction of closing the loop on today.

ShapeOne line weight (1.5–2px, round caps) draws everything. No filled cards: areas are marked by dot rows; where a container is needed it is a single pill outline. Keys, icons and tabs are circles.
TypeKumbh Sans throughout, a monoline geometric that matches the drawn line: 300 for the hero figure (58px phone, 104px desktop), 600 for rows and headings, tabular numerals.
DensityAiry to medium and symmetric: 20px gutters, 56–60px transaction rows, dot rows instead of rules, a kaavi band holding the header on every screen.
MotionThe kolam line draws itself once in 1.2s, ease-out, leaving today, rounding the fifteen dots and coming home to today; then today’s lamp ring settles in. Keys fill on press.
VoicePlain. Only “Day 15 of 31”, a three-item key (Gone, Today, To come) and “Each dot is 5% of the limit” are added, all true of the data.
SignatureThe month as a kolam: 31 dots (5-7-7-7-5), one continuous woven line that turns only at right angles and loops the 15 days gone, today ringed in lamp yellow. Budgets and goals reuse it as a 20-dot row.
